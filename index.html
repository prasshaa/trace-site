Early prototype · Product management in India

See what your experience proves for your next product role.

Trace is an AI career-pathway tool for product management in India. You upload your resume, and Trace shows what your experience proves for your next PM role, what’s missing, and a concrete plan to close the gap.

Contact

What Trace shows

  1. What your experience proves for your next PM role

  2. What’s missing

  3. A concrete plan to close the gap

It also keeps that case growing as your career does.

Who it’s for

  1. 01

    People moving into product (Associate PM or PM) from engineering, consulting, analytics, and so on.

  2. 02

    Associate PMs moving to PM.

  3. 03

    PMs moving to Senior PM.

Other moves are marked as limited support.

How it works

  1. 01

    Upload your resume

  2. 02

    Confirm your experience

  3. 03

    Pick your target role

  4. 04

    Get an evidence-cited readiness reading and a plan

Under the hood

  1. Semantic retrieval

    pgvector embeddings pick the relevant experience.

  2. Coaching-style reading

    An LLM writes it.

  3. Guardrails

    Every claim cites a line from your resume, never invents numbers, and respects negations like “did not own.”

Privacy

  • Your resume is used only for your own assessment.
  • Sharing is off by default.
  • You can export or delete your data.
  • Secondary use needs separate opt-in.

Status

Early prototype, built by Prathyusha Vedula (former product manager at MiQ on the Lab campaign management platform). Live demo opening soon.
